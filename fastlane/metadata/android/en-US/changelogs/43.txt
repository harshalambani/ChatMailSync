2.9.0: a new sync engine, written entirely in Kotlin. Needs Android 11 or
newer; phones on Android 7 to 10 stay on 2.2.0.

Photos and files sent with a caption are now attached. Cancel stops
promptly and is not counted as a failure. Each chat export is removed from
your phone once its sync completes. Restoring a backup is stricter. A new
Open-source licences screen is in Settings, and the privacy policy is
rewritten for this version.

Upgrading keeps your history, so nothing is mailed twice.
