One fix: restoring a backup no longer leaves the app saying you have none.

After you restored from a backup file, Home and Settings still showed the red
"No backup yet - a reset or a reinstall makes the app mail every chat again"
line, sitting directly above the history the restore had just protected. The
app now dates your cover from the backup you restored, so it only asks you to
make a new one when that file is genuinely getting old.
