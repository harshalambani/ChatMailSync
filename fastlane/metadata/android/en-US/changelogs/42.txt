2.2.0: the app now learns your name from a one-to-one export, so your own
messages land on the right side of the conversation without asking. A Me row
on the masthead and on each chat shows who the app thinks you are, with a
one-time banner the first time it works this out, and a place to set or
correct it by hand.

Setup is faster: four short steps end-to-end, easier app-password entry, and
the common providers (including AOL) listed first. Settings is reorganised
into Basic and Advanced sections, with polish across labels, back buttons and
chip wrapping.

Saving your mailbox and testing the connection can no longer hang the app --
both now return within a fixed time, every time, whether the network answers
or not.

The Windows desktop app is retired; this app is Android-only from here on.
