2.2.0: the app now learns your name from a one-to-one export, with a Me row
on the masthead and each chat, and a way to set or correct it by hand.

Setup is faster, with easier app-password entry. Settings is reorganised
into Basic and Advanced sections. Backup & restore has its own screen, with
a status pill showing whether it's up to date.

Saving and testing the connection can no longer hang the app.

The Windows desktop app is retired; this app is Android-only from here on.
