A cutoff date: never send me anything from before this.

Set a date and the app leaves everything older than it alone. Set it once in
Settings and it covers every chat, or set one on a single chat's own page and
that one wins for that chat.

It cannot cause a duplicate. For each chat the app uses whichever is later:
your cutoff, or the point that chat has already synced to -- so a chat already
archived up to March keeps March even under a cutoff of January. A message
stamped exactly on your cutoff day is still sent; the floor is that day's
midnight.

Held-back messages are counted as their own number in a run's detail, never
folded into "already there, so skipped", and the import preview now tells you
before you sync whether your cutoff would hold back all of a file, part of it,
or none of it.
