Two fixes to what the home screen shows you.

The watched folder no longer imports the photos and PDFs sitting beside an
export. Only .txt and .zip files are chats, and now every part of the app
agrees on that - the picker already did, the background import did not, so
attachments were being queued as if they were conversations.

The connection status at the top of every screen is now a real button. It
looks like one, so it should behave like one: tapping it opens your mail
account.
