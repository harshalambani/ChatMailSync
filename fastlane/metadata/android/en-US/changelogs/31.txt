Google sign-in has been removed. Chat Mail Sync now delivers over IMAP only,
using an app password you create with your mail provider. Any IMAP mailbox
works - Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud, Fastmail and others.

Existing users are unaffected: IMAP was already the default, and nothing about
how your chats are archived has changed. What is gone is a Google consent grant
the app no longer needed, and the account permissions that came with it.
