Your own messages are now shown as yours.

A WhatsApp export never marks which name is you - it writes your own
profile name the same way it writes everyone else's. Against a real
export this meant nothing matched, and the whole chat could render as if
every message came from the other person. The app now works out which
name is yours from a one-to-one export, with a Settings screen showing
what it found and letting you correct it.
