WhatsApp Chat Sync to Gmail

Private Gmail archive

Turns a WhatsApp "Export chat" file into a searchable, organised archive inside your own Gmail — nothing leaves your device except the messages you choose to sync, sent straight to your own mailbox.

What it does

  • Reads a WhatsApp chat export (text or zip with media) that you share or import.
  • Adds each message to your Gmail under a label named after the chat, grouped under WhatsApp.
  • Keeps photos, videos, and other attachments inline, just like the original conversation.
  • Skips anything already synced, so re-running it never duplicates messages.

What it never does

  • Never reads your existing Gmail — it only adds new messages and labels.
  • Never sends, deletes, or posts anything back to WhatsApp.
  • Never routes your messages through a server the developer controls — the app talks directly from your device to Google's Gmail API.
  • No ads, no analytics, no data sharing with anyone.

Available for Android (Kotlin/Compose, embedding the same Python sync engine used by the Windows desktop version) and Windows.
